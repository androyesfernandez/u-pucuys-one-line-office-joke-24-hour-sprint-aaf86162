## Drafts (all one line, target 180–200 chars)

D1 (fridge): Someone labelled their lunch 'DO NOT TOUCH - Dave, 2023', and honestly Dave's sandwich has now outlasted three managers, two restructures, and every single one of our quarterly goals.

D2 (microwave fish): Someone reheated fish in the office microwave at 9 a.m., and by 9:05 Facilities had logged a chemical incident, HR had opened a case, and the smell had already outlasted three managers.

D3 (badge): My access badge worked for four years, then stopped the morning I got promoted, which is the most honest performance review this company has ever given me, and the only one I ever agreed with.

D4 (booked room): The big meeting room is booked solid every day until March, yet it sits empty all afternoon, which means somebody is either a genius or just really, really committed to napping in there.

D5 (fire drill): The fire alarm went off and our whole floor evacuated in ninety seconds flat, which is the only deadline this company has ever hit, and it took an actual fire to do it, plus a very loud noise.

D6 (expenses): I filed a three-dollar stapler on expenses and it needed three approvals, a cost code, and a business justification, which is more governance than the three-million-dollar project ever got.

D7 (quick question): He stopped by my desk for a quick question, and forty minutes later I had a new project, two action items, and a calendar invite titled 'quick sync' to discuss the quick question over coffee.

D8 (leaving card): The leaving card for a man nobody had ever spoken to got forty-two signatures, a poem, and a whip-round, which is the most collaboration this team has managed all year, and he left on a Tuesday.

D9 (wifi): The Wi-Fi password changes every month and lives on a Post-it stuck to the router by the coffee machine, which IT calls security and the rest of us call the only onboarding document that ever worked.

D10 (mandatory fun): Our mandatory fun afternoon ended with a trust fall, a sprained wrist, and a risk assessment, which HR has now filed under 'team building' and I have filed under 'evidence' for the tribunal.

D11 (intern): The intern fixed in ten minutes the bug a steering committee had studied for a year, and the committee thanked her by inviting her to present it to the committee that studies committees.

D12 (goals): In January we set goals, in February we forgot them, and in December my review judges me against goals I set for a job I no longer have, in a team that no longer exists, by a manager I have never met.

D13 (hot desk): I booked a hot desk for the day and found someone else's family photo, a plant, and a nameplate, so I spent the morning working from the floor like a visiting consultant in my own job.

D14 (lift): The lift stops at every floor so strangers can ask me how my weekend was, and I have now told the same story to the same man four Mondays in a row, and he still asks like it is brand new.
